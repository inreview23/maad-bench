### Plain Statement of Empirical Patterns: Original 7 vs New 7 Sub-Datasets

1. **Loudness baseline vs median detector:**
   - On the original 7 (7 present), the loudness baseline beats the 25-detector median on 4 of 7 datasets:
     ravdess (0.900 vs 0.881), mimii (0.698 vs 0.613), physionet (0.593 vs 0.473), icbhi (0.499 vs 0.455).
     It fails to beat median on esc50 (0.654 vs 0.654), urbansound8k (0.636 vs 0.779), nsynth (0.494 vs 0.562).
   - On the new 7 (7 present), the loudness baseline beats the median detector on 4 of 7 datasets:
     cremad (0.832 vs 0.822), mimii_fan (0.789 vs 0.526), mimii_valve (0.548 vs 0.519), circor_rec (0.591 vs 0.560).
     It fails to beat median on mimii_slider (0.527 vs 0.681), dcase_toycar (0.496 vs 0.660), dcase_toyconveyor (0.480 vs 0.533).

2. **Best-to-median AUC gap:**
   - On the original 7, the best-to-median gap ranges from 0.031 to 0.327, with a mean gap of 0.096.
   - On the new 7, the best-to-median gap ranges from 0.011 to 0.354, with a mean gap of 0.146.
   - On Cremad (designated-class emotional speech, 91 folds), the best-to-median gap is tiny (0.011), with 25 of 25 detectors achieving AUC between 0.771 and 0.833.
   - In contrast, on machine sound datasets (mimii_fan gap: 0.354, dcase_toycar gap: 0.256), detector selection causes extreme performance divergence.

3. **Leading detector families on MIMII machine types (4 types):**
   - mimii: DIF (0.940); mimii_fan: DIF (0.880); mimii_slider: GMM (0.739); mimii_valve: COF (0.701).
   - Across the MIMII machine types, winners do NOT come from a single detector family:
     DIF (tree/isolation-based) leads on pump (0.940) and fan (0.880);
     GMM (probabilistic density) leads on slider (0.739, where DIF is 0.551);
     COF (connectivity-based distance) leads on valve (0.701, where DIF is 0.548 and GMM is 0.518).

4. **Leading detector families on DCASE machine types (2 types):**
   - dcase_toycar: KDE (0.916); dcase_toyconveyor: SOD (0.637).
   - KDE (kernel density estimation) leads on toycar (0.916, where SOD is 0.763);
     SOD (subspace outlier detection) leads on toyconveyor (0.637, where KDE is 0.544).
     Thus, the winning detector family does NOT transfer across machine types within the DCASE domain.
